What we do

HV / MV / LV electrical work

Power reaches a site in stages. In Malaysia the national grid carries it at high voltage (132 kV and above), steps it down to medium voltage (usually 11, 22 or 33 kV) for distribution, and finally to low voltage (400/230 V) for lighting, sockets and machines. We build that chain inside a site and make sure every part of it is tested before it is switched on.

Our scope

  • Medium and high voltage power systems
  • Low voltage power systems and switchboards
  • Power transformers
  • AMF boards and emergency main switchboards (EMSB)
  • Motor control centres (MCC)
  • MV and LV power cabling and cable support
  • Diesel generator systems
Read more on our services

ACMV

Air-conditioning and mechanical ventilation keeps a building at the right temperature, humidity and air quality. A typical system pairs chillers or condensing units with air handling units, fan coil units, ducting and exhaust fans. Behind each one sits electrical work: power supply, motor starters and control panels, and links to the building automation system that schedules and monitors it all.

Our scope

  • HVAC and ventilation systems
  • Power and controls for chiller plant and air handling units
  • Building automation system (BAS) and SCADA
  • Testing and commissioning
Read more on our services

Fire fighting

Active fire protection detects a fire, raises the alarm and fights it until help arrives: sprinklers, hose reels, hydrants, wet and dry risers, fire pumps, and the alarm and detection systems that set them off. In Malaysia these systems follow the Uniform Building By-Laws and need approval from the Fire and Rescue Department (BOMBA). The electrical side matters as much as the pipework: fire pump control panels, alarm panels, and power supplies that must keep running when everything else is off.

Our scope

  • Fire fighting system installations for buildings and plants
  • Power supply and controls for fire pumps and alarm panels
Discuss a project with us

ELV systems

Extra-low voltage covers systems that run below 50 V AC (or 120 V DC): the networks that carry data, pictures and sound rather than power. CCTV, access control, structured cabling and data networks, telephones, public address and AV, and SMATV for shared satellite and TV reception are all ELV. They are installed alongside the power systems, so planning both together saves rework later.

Our scope

  • CCTV
  • SMATV
  • AV and PA systems
  • Telecommunication internal and infrastructure works
  • ICT solutions and data networking
Read more on our services

Renovation & restoration

Electrical installations age: insulation wears, loads grow and older switchboards fall behind current standards. Renovation brings an existing building up to date without starting over, from rewiring and switchboard replacement to capacity upgrades, while restoration of older buildings means fitting new services carefully around the original structure. KLS began in 1984 as a master rewiring service provider, so this is where we started.

Our scope

  • Rewiring of existing installations
  • Switchboard replacement and upgrades
  • Capacity upgrades for added load
  • Restoration works for older buildings
