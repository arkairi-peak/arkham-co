Getting here

EESOY sits within the Kota Kemuning residential and retail strip, a short drive from the Shah Alam Expressway (KESAS).

By car

Take the Kota Kemuning exit off KESAS and head toward Jalan Anggerik Vanilla; the outlet is on the ground floor of the retail row.

By e-hailing

Set the drop-off point to "EESOY, Jalan Anggerik Vanilla AB 31/AB" to avoid confusion with the nearby residential blocks.

Delivery

Available through major delivery apps within a limited radius; search "EESOY" to check if your area is covered.
